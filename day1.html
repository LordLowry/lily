Travel to Spain

Wednesday, 27th May 2026

⏰ Spain is 1 hour ahead of Irish time. When it's 3pm in Dublin, it's 4pm in Spain. Adjust your watch on departure!
🛫 Outbound Flight
  • 📍From: Dublin Airport, Terminal 2
  • 📍To: Málaga Airport
  • 🔢Flight: EI586 (Aer Lingus)
  • 🕓Departs: 16:10  ·  Arrives: 20:20 (local)
  • 🕑Check-in opens: 14:10 (2 hrs before)
📱 Check-in Info

Your airline booking reference (PNR) will be emailed to you two days before departure (i.e. Monday 25 May) with online check-in instructions.

If you have trouble with online check-in, you can check in at the airport and get boarding cards printed there — no problem at all.

Your 10kg checked bag may not appear in the online booking until one day before departure — don't worry, it has been added to your booking if you paid for it.

🧳 Baggage Allowance
  • 👜Hand bag: 1 small bag only — must fit under the seat (25×33×20cm)
  • 🧳Checked bag: 1 × 10kg included. Max size: 55×40×24cm
  • ⚠️Oversize: Extra bags over allowance cost €35 each way, paid at the gate
🚌 Arrival & Transfer

After landing and collecting your luggage, head to the arrivals area where a Travel Department representative will be waiting holding a Travel Department sign.

The coach transfer to the hotel takes approximately 1 hour 15 minutes.

🍽️ Evening Meal

Due to the late arrival time, your first dinner at the hotel will be a cold platter. From Day 2 onwards, full hot dinners are included each evening.

📞 In Case of Delay

If you have any issues at the airport or on arrival, contact the local team via the emergency numbers on the Trip Overview page, or reach your guide Claire directly: 00 34 678 686 088
