🚌 Day Trip to Granada

Today you travel to the magnificent city of Granada for a guided tour — one of the most spectacular destinations in all of Spain, nestled at the foot of the Sierra Nevada mountains.

🌿 Generalife Gardens

The Generalife was the summer palace and gardens of the Nasrid rulers. Its beautiful terraced gardens, fountains and courtyards are among the most celebrated in the world — a breathtaking sea of colour and calm.

👑 La Alhambra

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Spain's greatest treasures — the Alhambra is a palace and fortress complex built by the Moorish rulers of Granada in the 13th and 14th centuries.

With magnificent courtyards, intricate geometric tilework, carved stucco arches, and stunning mountain views, it is truly one of the most beautiful places in Europe.

  • 🎟️ General entrance included Included
  • ⚠️ Please note: Entry to the Nasrid Palaces (the most famous rooms) is not guaranteed — subject to availability. Your guide will advise on the day.
📍 About Granada

Granada was the last Moorish kingdom in Spain, falling to the Catholic Monarchs in 1492 — the same year Columbus sailed to the Americas. Its unique blend of Moorish and Spanish heritage makes it utterly unlike anywhere else.
