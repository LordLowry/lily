🌿 Stop 1 – El Valle de Lecrín

A hidden gem — a beautiful valley well off the beaten track that feels frozen in time. Almost entirely untouched by tourism, this peaceful area retains its authentic Andalucían charm.

You'll visit Vélez de Benaudalla, a town in the heart of the countryside, and explore a fascinating museum dedicated to olive oil production — the region is famous for it. Taste some of the local olive oil before moving on!

🏖️ Stop 2 – Salobreña

A charming coastal town with lovely beaches and pleasant year-round temperatures. Enjoy some free time to walk along the seafront or explore at your own pace.

🏘️ Stop 3 – Frigiliana

Often voted one of the prettiest villages in Spain, Frigiliana is a dazzling white hilltop village with flower-lined narrow streets and stunning views.

  • 🍷 Wine tasting at a quaint family-run bodega — including the special dessert wine made only in Frigiliana Included
  • 🍞 Taste local specialities: fig bread and sugar-cane honey Included
  • 🚶 Gentle stroll down the narrow, flower-lined cobbled streets
  • 🌸 Free time at leisure in the village before returning to the hotel
📍 About Frigiliana

Frigiliana is perched in the hills above Nerja and is one of Andalucía's most celebrated white villages (pueblos blancos). Its winding lanes are draped in colourful flowers and the views out to sea are breathtaking.

The village is hilly with uneven cobblestone surfaces — take your time and enjoy the atmosphere.
