🗺️ Morning – Guided Tour of Nerja

A short drive from the hotel brings you to the charming town of Nerja for a guided walking tour along its beautiful promenade.

  • 🏛️ Balcón de Europa — the famous viewpoint named by King Alfonso XII, with sweeping Mediterranean sea views
  • 🌊 Stroll along the promenade overlooking the sparkling Costa del Sol coastline
  • 🥂 Tapas & drinks in a local bar in town Included
🌅 Afternoon – Free Time

The afternoon is yours to enjoy at your own pace. You might wander the streets of Nerja, browse the local shops, find a café by the sea, or simply relax and soak up the sunshine.

📍 About Nerja

Nerja is one of the most picturesque towns on the Costa del Sol — a whitewashed gem perched on cliffs above pristine beaches. It has a wonderful blend of authentic Andalucían charm and beautiful natural scenery.
