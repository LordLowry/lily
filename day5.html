🎨 Málaga is the birthplace of Pablo Picasso — one of the greatest artists of the 20th century. Today you'll see where he came into the world!
🚌 Full Day Excursion – Málaga

A full day out exploring Andalucía's second biggest city — vibrant, cultured and full of history.

🏔️ Gibralfaro Viewpoint

The panoramic coach tour takes you up to the top of Gibralfaro hill — the site of a Moorish castle — for breathtaking views over Málaga, its beautiful harbour and the Mediterranean beyond.

🖼️ Picasso Museum & Birthplace

Set in a stunning Renaissance palace, the Museo Picasso Málaga houses an impressive collection of his works. You'll also visit Picasso's birthplace — the house where he was born in 1881.

You are free to visit the museum and explore at your own pace.

⛪ Málaga Cathedral

Known locally as "La Manquita" (the one-armed lady) due to its famously unfinished second tower, Málaga's Renaissance cathedral is one of the most striking in Andalucía. Free time to explore inside and around it.

ℹ️ Good to Know

Some entrance fees may not be included — check with your guide Claire on the day. The general entrance to the Alhambra area is included on Day 7 but today's sites (cathedral interior, museum entry) may have a small charge.
